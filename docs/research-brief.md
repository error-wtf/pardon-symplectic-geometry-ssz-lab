# Research Brief: John Pardon and the 2026 Fields Medal

## Summary

John Pardon received a 2026 Fields Medal for major contributions to geometry and topology. The IMU citation highlights symplectic geometry, virtual fundamental cycles, Fukaya categories, counting holomorphic curves, group actions on 3-manifolds, and knot theory.

## Background

Pardon is a professor at the Simons Center for Geometry and Physics at Stony Brook University. Institutional biographies note his Princeton A.B. in 2011, Stanford Ph.D. in 2015 under Yakov Eliashberg, and later positions at Stanford, Princeton, and Stony Brook/Simons Center.

## Why The Work Matters

Many geometric problems ask for invariants: quantities that stay stable under allowed deformations. In symplectic geometry and topology, such invariants often come from counting geometric objects such as holomorphic curves. Pardon's work develops machinery that makes such counts possible and coherent in difficult settings.

## Scope Boundary

The repository is educational. It explains background concepts and provides numerical toy models, but it does not reproduce the research-level constructions.
