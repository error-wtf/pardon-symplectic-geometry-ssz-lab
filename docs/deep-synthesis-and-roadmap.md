# Deep Synthesis: Pardon's Mathematics, SSZ, and the Local Physics Repos

## Central Finding

The useful bridge is not a claim that John Pardon proves SSZ. SSZ already has Lagrange/Hamilton, geodesic, trajectory, orbit and phi-eigenmode repositories. That makes symplectic validation and degeneracy-aware testing directly useful.
